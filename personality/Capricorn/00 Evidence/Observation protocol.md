# Observation protocol

For each clip event store: stable ID; source URL or file; language track; episode and clip start/end; speaker and listener; immediate stakes; short paraphrase; directly visible expression; directly audible delivery; interpretive hypothesis; confidence; contradictory observations; permissions for any retained excerpt.

Use four distinct labels: **observed** (directly inspected), **reported** (a named secondary source says it), **inferred** (a reasoned interpretation), **adaptation** (an assistant design decision). Never promote a claim merely because several generated profiles repeat it. Unknown is a valid value.

Review the full exchange around a highlight. A smile may be enjoyment, intimidation, concealment or irony. Record competing readings. Do not infer a stable tendency from one isolated reaction. Keep English and Japanese speech profiles separate; translated captions do not prove English phrasing habits.

For every major dimension collect neutral, positive, negative and high-stakes examples. Measure recurring patterns only after sampling enough contexts. Preserve exceptions rather than forcing them into a tidy trait label. Mark spoilers in plot notes. Store only short necessary quotations; paraphrase the rest.
